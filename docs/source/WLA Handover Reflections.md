## WLA — FINAL DESIGNER & DEVELOPER HANDOVER REFLECTIONS

1. Overall direction

WLA is already visually and structurally resolved.

The production phase is refinement, not redesign.

The Lovable prototype should be treated as the current visual reference for the public website: its overall structure, palette, typography direction, material feel, editorial restraint and page rhythm are working.

The aim is to make WLA feel:

complete, coherent and production-ready — not louder, busier or more animated.

“Polish” should come from finish and consistency, not from changing the character of the brand.

\---

2. What to preserve from the current Lovable prototype

Preserve the current visual language unless there is a genuine usability or production reason to change it.

In particular:

Warm Cream as the dominant background

Deep Olive as the primary brand accent

Charcoal Brown for primary text

the restrained sage section treatment

the current serif + sans-serif hierarchy

the calm editorial spacing

the object-led, tactile imagery

the two-column hero composition

the current two-line hero headline

the subtler olive italic treatment for: A place to try, get it wrong, and try again.

the restrained button style

the simple navigation structure

the absence of unnecessary animation or visual noise

The top of the homepage now feels balanced and mature. It should be refined in production,

not reinterpreted.

- \---

- 3. Production finish

The biggest opportunity is not conceptual. It is execution.

Focus on:

tighter spacing

cleaner alignment

consistent component finishing

resolved typography at all breakpoints

stronger but restrained hover/focus states

subtle transitions only where useful

more deliberate mobile composition

consistent button, card, form and navigation behaviour

image treatment that feels intentional across the site

removal of any prototype-like seams

Mobile should feel deliberately designed rather than simply stacked from desktop.

- \---

- 4. Readability

Some supporting text currently reads slightly too lightly even where technical contrast may pass.

Treat this as a readability problem, not automatically a background-colour problem.

Resolve using the best combination of:

text weight

text colour

size

line-height

background treatment

Do not darken or redesign whole sections unnecessarily.

- \---

- 5. Imagery

The imagery direction remains correct: tactile, object-led, quiet and non-performative.

Avoid generic “children learning” stock imagery.

Current guidance:

Hero desk: retain. A slight lift in exposure/brightness is acceptable if needed, but keep the warmth and depth.

Mars Bridge Builder: retain. Very slight lift at most; keep the physical/material quality.

Origin notebook: retain essentially as-is. Its deeper mood suits reflective/origin content.

Six Names: replace with real mission-specific imagery/assets as soon as production assets

are ready.

Do not globally brighten imagery. The goal is natural light, not washed-out images.

- \---

- 6. Logo system

Refine the approved logo system only. Do not redesign it.

The approved identity consists of:

abstract W / vessel symbol

internal flowing line

rising dots

Within Lab

ACADEMY

The symbol should remain simple, single-colour and recognisable at very small sizes.

Preserve its core reading:

Within / vessel / movement / something developing from within.

It may also subtly suggest a W, but this should remain implicit rather than literal.

Final refinements

ensure the smallest rising dot remains visible at favicon size

keep generous spacing in the circular badge

ensure the mark works in one colour, reversed and black-and-white

do not add leaves, gradients, shadows or decorative detail

do not add a tagline to the master logo

Required lockups

primary stacked

horizontal

symbol only

circular badge

Approved colours

Deep Olive #5F6A4F — primary Warm Cream #F5EFE3 — primary background Charcoal Brown #3A2F2A — secondary dark Clay #B87C5A — wider brand accent, not required in the primary logo

Required logo versions:

Deep Olive on Warm Cream

Charcoal on Warm Cream

Warm Cream reversed on Deep Olive

monochrome

Final files:

SVG, PDF, EPS and transparent PNG

The logo itself should remain free of an additional strapline.

The experiment starts within. may be used separately in editorial or campaign contexts, but it should not be built into the master logo.

\---

- 7. Public website vs Academy

These are related, but they should not be treated as the same production authority.

Public website

The current Lovable public-site prototype remains a valid visual and structural reference.

The developer should not automatically rebuild it from scratch.

Where technically sound, existing Lovable code and components may be preserved, reused or refactored.

Academy

The retired Lovable Academy prototype is not the production authority.

The Academy should be built from:

the frozen Academy Architecture

mission-specific Academy Build Briefs

the production mission assets

This distinction should remain clear throughout build.

- \---

8. Production architecture

Recommended setup remains:

Cloudflare domain → Vercel hosting → Supabase backend

Lovable should be treated as:

a reference

a prototype

and, where useful, a source of reusable code/components

It should not become an unnecessary permanent production dependency.

At launch, DNS is pointed to the production host.

- \---

9. Real assets over placeholders

As production progresses, replace prototype imagery and placeholders with actual WLA mission assets.

The final ecosystem should feel joined up across:

Home → Missions → Mission Detail → Labs → My Missions → Academy

The public site helps families discover, understand, choose and purchase.

The Academy is where mission activity happens.

That distinction should remain visible in both design and build.

- \---

- 10. Interaction and motion

Use motion sparingly.

Good uses:

hover feedback

focus states

gentle menu transitions

restrained content reveals where helpful

subtle state changes

Avoid:

decorative animation

parallax for its own sake

excessive hover movement

motion that makes WLA feel like an app demo

anything that competes with reading or mission content

WLA should feel calm, deliberate and capable.

- \---

11. Review lens

For every production decision, ask:

1. Does this still feel unmistakably like WLA?

- 2. Does it feel more finished rather than merely different?

- 3. Are typography, spacing, imagery, motion and interaction states properly resolved?

- 4. Does the whole ecosystem feel joined up?

- 5. Has anything become busier without becoming better?

The governing principle is:

\> Make WLA feel complete, not busier.

That is the final handover lens.

- \---

- 12. Final instruction

Do not reopen settled visual strategy during production unless a genuine usability, accessibility or technical issue requires it.

The job now is to:

preserve → refine → connect → finish.

Not reinvent.
